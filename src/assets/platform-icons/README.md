# Account platform icons

The QQ and Discord SVGs are bundled locally from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), pinned to revision `1089fb7d2bf0e323f834c205ab76265005a6d5e8`. Their paths are unchanged.

- [QQ source](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/1089fb7d2bf0e323f834c205ab76265005a6d5e8/icons/qq.svg)
- [Discord source](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/1089fb7d2bf0e323f834c205ab76265005a6d5e8/icons/discord.svg)

Simple Icons distributes the collection under CC0 1.0; the upstream license is preserved in `LICENSE.simple-icons.md`. Platform names and logos remain trademarks of their respective owners. These icons identify user-created account entries and registered-platform tags.

Google, Microsoft, GitHub, Apple, Notion and OpenAI assets are imported from the existing `@lobehub/icons-static-svg` dependency (MIT). YouTube uses the existing `@mui/icons-material` component. No external image requests are made at runtime.
